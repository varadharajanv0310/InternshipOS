# Ingestion attribution

Company seed rows are a curated subset of colophon-group/jobseek at commit 4bf9a672b271841c034089b4464ea2210a2dc966. Import metadata and candidate association status are retained; known inaccurate candidate mappings were excluded.

Adapters are independent implementations informed by the public protocols and research of kalil0321/ats-scrapers and strelov1/freehire. No anti-bot or account/session components are used.

Fixtures contain a bounded sample of publicly observed job data solely for parser regression checks; original source ownership and dates remain in the payloads. Their presence is not an endorsement or assertion that the listings remain open.
