# Third-party source notes

The curated company seed registry uses candidate mappings from colophon-group/jobseek, snapshot 4bf9a672b271841c034089b4464ea2210a2dc966. Employer facts and source associations are labelled separately. The optional Python JobSpy dependency is licensed MIT; package distributions retain its notices. Public ATS/board data is collected by the original InternshipOS adapters; Freehire responses are supplemental observations rather than a bundled upstream product. Detailed inspected-project provenance remains in research sources.
